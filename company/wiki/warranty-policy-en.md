## Veltra Power Warranty Policy, edition June 2026

1. **Warranty period.** Veltra HX-50 inverters are covered for five (5) years from the date of delivery.
2. **Claim notice.** The buyer must notify Veltra Power of a defect in writing within fourteen (14) calendar days of detecting it. Claims notified later may be rejected.
3. **Remedy.** After diagnostics, Veltra Power replaces the defective unit within ten (10) business days of confirming the defect.
4. **Exclusions.** Transport damage after handover to the carrier, incorrect installation, grid overvoltage outside tolerance, unauthorised repair.
5. **Installation.** Installation by trained personnel is a condition of the warranty.
